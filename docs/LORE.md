# World Lore & Narrative Setting — Adventure MNC

## Timeline: Year 2091

The Mooncrust Nova Corporation (MNC) maintains an unyielding monopoly over volatile resource extraction across the Lunar South Pole—predominantly water ice traps within permanently shadowed craters and Helium-3 deposits along the sunlit ridges of the Malapert Massif and Shackleton crater rim.

## The Player Character

You operate as an unbonded freelance contractor—courier, wildcat miner, smuggler—subsisting in the legal void between MNC-chartered extraction domes and unregistered survival encampments sunk into deep crater floors.

## Corporate Security

In the vacuum of the lunar frontier, there are no civil law enforcement agencies. MNC maintains Corporate Security ("CorpSec"):
- Automated surveillance relays and reconnaissance drones.
- Kinetic enforcement patrols equipped with specialized non-explosive kinetic slugs and EMP disruption nets.
- Response Escalation: Alert Level 1 (tracking & airspace warning) through Alert Level 5 (full airlock lockout, orbital telemetry jamming, CorpSec interceptor deployment).

## Environmental Realism

The Moon is a silent, lethal adversary:
- Absolute vacuum: Sound conducts exclusively through physical contact with the suit or via internal suit life-support telemetry.
- Severe thermal divergence: Extremes ranging from +120 °C under direct solar radiation to −230 °C in permanently shadowed regions (PSR).
- Electrostatic abrasive regolith: Fine-grained volcanic dust that adheres to suit seals and optical visors, degrading thermal dissipation and mobility.
